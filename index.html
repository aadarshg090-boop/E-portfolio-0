Curiosity / discipline / creation

Student. Debater. Creative. Builder.

I am a middle-school student drawn to ideas that cross boundaries—technology, entrepreneurship, debate, storytelling and design. I like learning how a product works, asking how it could be better, then testing my own version through projects and experiments.

Debate gives me structure for research, reasoning and communication. Video editing and presentation work help me turn raw ideas into clear stories. AI and product exploration give me a space to experiment with tools, prototypes and ambitious concepts.

My current startup exploration is Setyn, a privacy-first AI infrastructure concept focused on local and private compute, AI models, workflows and user control. It is a developing idea—not a launched company.

05 / The personal thesis

Why I.

The motivations underneath my interests—more than a list of accomplishments.

01 — Curiosity

Because questions
should lead somewhere.

I want to understand the systems behind things—not just use them. When something feels inefficient or exciting, I start exploring how it works and what I could make from it.

02 — Building

Because ideas
deserve a prototype.

I enjoy moving from imagination to something tangible: a video, a presentation, a product interface, a startup concept or a small experiment. The first version is a starting point.

03 — Expression

Because clarity
is a craft.

Debate and editing have taught me that the way an idea is framed matters. I like finding the story, shaping the message and making complex things understandable.

04 — Growth

Because I am
not finished.

I am early in my journey. I want to keep entering rooms where I am the least experienced, learn from people who are better, and turn feedback into progress instead of pretending I already know it all.

Not a finished story.
A direction.
